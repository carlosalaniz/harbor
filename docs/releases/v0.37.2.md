# Harbor 0.37.2

A calmer Publishing page.

- **Addresses are grouped by how people reach them.** *On the internet* lists your domains, one row per app with
  every name it has, and a domain that is not reachable moves to the top with its reason. A working address shows
  just its link; the state and the last check appear only when something is wrong.
- **The tailnet is one compact card.** Your tailnet's hostname sits in its header, the Harbor console has its switch
  there (it was listed twice before), and each app shows as its port. *Choose apps…* opens the bulk picker in
  Settings → Remote access.
- **Not published** lists the running apps that have no address yet. An app that already has one is managed from
  its row (*Manage*) instead of appearing a second time.

Nothing else changes: same addresses, same Publish dialog, no new API.
