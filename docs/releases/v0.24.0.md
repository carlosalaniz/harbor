## Harbor 0.24.0 — encrypt an app you already have, change its passphrase

Everything in the [0.23.0 notes](https://github.com/carlosalaniz/harbor/blob/main/docs/releases/v0.23.0.md)
still applies. New in this release:

- **Encrypt an existing app** — app details → *Encrypt this app…*, or `harbor seal <app>`. It stays
  the same app (same name, ports, addresses, links and secrets). Its data moves into a sealed home on
  this machine that unlocks when you log in. The app is down during the copy. The plain copy is deleted
  only after the encrypted app has started; if anything fails first, the app keeps running unencrypted
  as before. Needs free space for one extra copy.
- **Change an app's passphrase** — app details → *Change passphrase…*, or `harbor passphrase <app>`.
  Switch between your own passphrase and Harbor's own key in either direction. The data is not
  re-encrypted, so it takes a second. Choosing your own passphrase gives the app its own 12 recovery
  words once.
- **Fixed: "Uninstall completely" failed for encrypted apps** (`Permission denied`). Harbor now
  deletes the app's encrypted folder with the right permissions.

Already running Harbor? Settings → Overview → Update.
